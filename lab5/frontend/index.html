INDIAN SPACE RESEARCH ORGANISATION

Exploring Space.
Serving India.

The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is India's national space agency, dedicated to space research, exploration and the development of space technology.

About ISRO

ISRO develops and operates satellites, launch vehicles and space-based applications for communication, navigation, Earth observation, scientific research and planetary exploration.

Our Mission

Space Technology

Developing advanced launch vehicles, satellites and space systems.

Scientific Research

Exploring Earth, the Moon, planets and the universe through scientific missions.

National Development

Using space technology for communication, agriculture, weather forecasting and disaster management.

ISRO Journey

1969

Indian Space Research Organisation was established.

1975

Aryabhata, India's first satellite, was launched.

1980

SLV-3 successfully placed the Rohini satellite into orbit.

2014

Mars Orbiter Mission successfully entered Mars orbit.

2023

Chandrayaan-3 successfully achieved a soft landing on the Moon.
