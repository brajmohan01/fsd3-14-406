Indian Space Research Organisation

Exploring Space.

ISRO is India's national space agency, dedicated to the development and application of space technology for national development and scientific exploration.

About ISRO

The Indian Space Research Organisation develops space technologies and applications covering communication, navigation, earth observation, meteorology, space science and planetary exploration.

01

Space Technology

Developing launch vehicles, satellites and advanced space systems for a wide range of applications.

02

Scientific Research

Studying the Earth, Moon, planets and the wider universe through scientific missions.

03

National Development

Applying space technology to communication, agriculture, disaster management and other areas.

Journey Through Time

1969

ISRO Established

The Indian Space Research Organisation was established to develop India's space programme.

1975

First Indian Satellite

Aryabhata became India's first satellite.

1980

First Indian Launch Vehicle Success

SLV-3 successfully placed the Rohini satellite into orbit.

2014

Mars Orbiter Mission

India's Mars Orbiter Mission successfully entered Mars orbit.

2023

Chandrayaan-3

Chandrayaan-3 successfully achieved a soft landing on the lunar surface.
