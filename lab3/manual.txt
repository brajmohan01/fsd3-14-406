Space is the vast, seemingly endless region beyond Earth's atmosphere. It contains planets, stars, moons, galaxies, asteroids, comets, and many other fascinating objects. Scientists study space to understand how the universe was formed, how celestial bodies work, and whether life may exist beyond Earth.

Our solar system is located in the Milky Way galaxy and consists of the Sun, eight planets, their moons, and many smaller objects. Space exploration has helped humans learn more about distant worlds through telescopes, satellites, space probes, and human missions.
